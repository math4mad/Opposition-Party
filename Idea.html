Here is a comprehensive peer-review of the paper “Concept Formation and Alignment in Language Models: Bridging Statistical Patterns in Latent Space to Concept Taxonomy”.

Peer Review Report

1. Overview and Summary

This paper investigates the internal conceptual representations of Language Models (LMs), specifically addressing the debate of whether LMs merely memorize statistical co-occurrences (“stochastic parrots”) or form structured, human-aligned concepts. The authors propose a novel, probe-agnostic methodology using K-NN graph construction, UMAP-based fuzzy weighting, and Louvain community detection to extract hierarchical concept taxonomies directly from the models’ latent spaces.

By examining GloVe, ALBERT, and T5, the authors demonstrate that concepts form hierarchically and align significantly across different architectures (LM-LM alignment) and with human-defined taxonomies (Human-LM alignment). Notably, the paper finds that rich conceptual structures (e.g., geographical “mental maps”, numerical orderliness, and social structures) emerge as early as the input embedding layer. Finally, the authors present preliminary evidence of “Knowledge-Reasoning Separation,” showing that granular semantic knowledge can be obscured (via centroid replacement) without severely degrading performance on standard NLU benchmarks.

2. Major Strengths

  • Novel and Robust Methodology: The decision to avoid traditional “probing” classifiers is a major strength. Probes often conflate the model’s inherent representations with the probe’s own learning capacity. By framing concept extraction as a topological graph problem (community detection), the authors provide a more organic, unsupervised view of the semantic manifold that does not require predefined cluster counts.
  • Fascinating Empirical Insights: The paper yields several highly interpretable and intriguing findings:
    • Geographical Mental Maps (Figure 2): The discovery that the model organizes locations and names with an implicit East-to-West spatial ordering is a profound indicator of distributional semantics capturing real-world topology.
    • Numerical Orderliness (Table 3): Proving that numbers are not just clustered by magnitude but possess local topological “orderliness” on the manifold is a significant contribution to numerical cognition in LMs.
    • Bias Detection (Table 5): The observation that “God/divinity” clusters with Christianity, while Islam/Judaism form separate clusters, elegantly demonstrates how this methodology can be used for auditing societal and cultural biases in pre-training data.
  • Cross-Model Generalization: Evaluating LM-LM alignment (Table 2) across fundamentally different architectures (statistical GloVe, Encoder-only ALBERT, Encoder-Decoder T5) strengthens the claim that concept formation is a fundamental property of language distribution rather than an artifact of a specific Transformer architecture.
  • Practical Implications for Token Engineering: The “Knowledge-Reasoning Separation” experiment (Table 6) opens highly promising avenues for Machine Unlearning, privacy preservation, and debiasing. If factual/sensitive knowledge is localized in the input embedding layer and separable from syntactic reasoning, targeted “embedding surgery” becomes viable without catastrophic forgetting.

3. Critical Weaknesses and Areas for Improvement

While the paper is thought-provoking, there are several methodological and conceptual limitations that need to be addressed:

  • Over-reliance on the Input Embedding Layer: The authors focus almost exclusively on the input embedding layer (Section 3.4), arguing that early concept formation hints at “semantic memory.” However, input embeddings in Transformers are context-independent lookup tables. While they capture static, distributional semantics (similar to GloVe), they do not capture contextualized reasoning, polysemy resolution, or compositional semantics, which emerge in the deeper Transformer layers. Equating static input embeddings to the model’s complete “conceptual understanding” or “semantic memory” is a philosophical leap. The authors should investigate how these communities evolve or dissolve in the deeper, contextualized layers.
  • Flawed Evaluation of “Knowledge-Reasoning Separation”: In Section 4.3, the authors replace community members with their centroid and observe minimal performance drops on GLUE/SuperGLUE/SQUAD. They conclude that granular pre-trained knowledge is not required for reasoning. However, GLUE and SuperGLUE are primarily Natural Language Understanding (NLU) and syntactic reasoning tasks, not factual knowledge retrieval tasks.
    • Critique: If you replace “James”, “John”, and “Alex” with a single centroid, the model still recognizes the token as a “human noun,” which is all that is needed for NLI or sentiment analysis. The model’s factual knowledge is destroyed, but GLUE doesn’t test factual knowledge. To truly prove “separation,” the authors must evaluate this intervention on Fact-Retrieval benchmarks (e.g., LAMA, TriviaQA) or knowledge-intensive generation tasks.
  • The Tokenization Artifact Problem: The paper acknowledges subword tokenization as a limitation, but it fundamentally threatens the validity of the “concepts” being extracted. SentencePiece/BPE fragments rare words, names, and locations into subword tokens. A cluster might not represent a “concept” (e.g., German Cities), but rather a morphological artifact (e.g., tokens ending in “-burg” or “-heim”). The authors attempt to filter by whitespace, but this is insufficient. A deeper analysis separating whole-word tokens from subword fragments is necessary to prove the clusters are semantic, not merely morphological.
  • Subjectivity in “Social Structures” Annotation: Using GPT-4 to label and classify the “Social Structures” cluster (Table 5) introduces circularity and bias. GPT-4 has its own alignment biases and conceptual taxonomies. Using an LLM to evaluate the conceptual alignment of another LM risks measuring “LM-to-LLM alignment” rather than true “Human-LM alignment.”

4. Questions and Suggestions for the Authors

  1. Contextual vs. Static: How do the communities identified in the input embedding layer compare to those formed using the hidden states of the final Transformer layer? Do concepts become more refined, or do they blend into task-specific representations?
  2. Fact-Retrieval Impact: Could you run the “mid-point” centroid replacement experiment on a Knowledge Probe dataset (like LAMA)? It is highly likely that factual accuracy would drop to near zero, which would nuance your claim about “Knowledge-Reasoning Separation.”
  3. Subword Analysis: What percentage of the tokens in the “Names and Locations” clusters are partial subwords (e.g., “##burg”, “##son”)? Please provide a breakdown to assure the reader that the model is clustering concepts, not just byte-pair statistics.
  4. Computational Complexity: Building a K-NN graph and running Louvain on a 30,000+ token vocabulary in high-dimensional space is computationally expensive. Please include a brief section on the time complexity and hardware requirements of your extraction pipeline.

5. Conclusion

Rating: Strong Accept (with minor revisions)

This is a highly innovative and compelling paper that shifts the interpretability paradigm from linear probing to topological manifold analysis. The discovery of implicit geographical maps and numerical orderliness in latent spaces provides strong evidence against the pure “stochastic parrot” hypothesis, showing that LMs do build structured, human-aligned mental maps of the world.

While the claims regarding “Knowledge-Reasoning Separation” require more rigorous benchmarking (specifically using fact-retrieval tasks rather than just GLUE), the methodology and the core findings regarding concept formation and alignment are robust, well-documented, and highly valuable to the Mechanistic Interpretability and XAI communities. This work lays an excellent foundation for future “Token Engineering” and model editing.

一、高效之处

1. 发现成本低（提取复杂度近线性） - 最近邻下降（NN-descent）建 K-NN 图约 O(N log N)，Louvain 社区检测近线性，标签传播 O(E)；相比层次聚类 O(N² log N)、谱聚类 O(N³)，在 3 万 token 规模上完全可扩展。 - 无需预设簇数，靠模ularity自适应——这正好匹配”概念数量未知”的先验。 - 代价：论文为规避 Louvain 聚合阶段权重失真（见 §3.3），改用多粒度 k∈{100…6} 迭代重跑 K-NN，等于用重复计算换层次保真度。这是一个明确的 trade-off。

2. 压缩/率失真效率高（岛 = 天然向量量化） - 岛结构等价于一个codebook：质心是码字，成员是残差。Table 6 的 mid-point 实验是最直接的证据——把整个社区成员替换为质心后，GLUE/SuperGLUE/SQuAD 几乎不降甚至略升。说明岛内冗余极高，岛状组织的可压缩率很好。 - 同时带来编辑局部性：改一座岛不波及全局，这正是 machine unlearning、去偏、隐私擦除想要的高效基底（论文”token engineering”的卖点）。

3. 检索/导航效率高（层次岛 = coarse-to-fine 索引） - 先定位超社区、再岛内细化，可剪枝搜索空间；这与 ANN 检索中 HNSW/IVF 索引利用流形聚类结构的原理同构——聚类岛状结构本身就是对数级导航的前提。 - Table 2 的跨模型社区映射（T5↔︎ALBERT 细粒度社区映射率高达 0.97）说明岛可以作为跨模型知识迁移的对齐单位，比逐 token 对齐便宜得多。

4. 学习效率高（先验前置） - 概念在输入嵌入层就已成形，等于把”类别先验”写进了 lookup table；深层 attention 不必从零重建类别，只做上下文路由与组合。静态岛负责”它是什么”，动态注意力负责”此处取哪个义项”——分工即效率。这也与认知科学中原型/范畴化编码的压缩优势一致。

二、不高效之处（固有代价）

  • 多义词失效：硬分区迫使一个 token 只属一座岛，而 “bank”/“apple” 需要多重成员身份。论文只在建图边权上借了 UMAP 的模糊权重，输出仍是硬社区——模糊性被丢掉了，真要多义高效需软/重叠社区。
  • 模ularity分辨率极限：Louvain 在大图上看不见小社区，小島会被大岛吞并；论文用多 k 迭代补偿，又回到算力代价。
  • 各向异性与 hub 词：嵌入空间整体呈锥状各向异性，高频词成为连接诸岛的 hub，污染 K-NN 图、削弱岛的可分性。
  • 分布驱动 ≠ 任务驱动：岛的粒度由语料频率决定（英文名形成超社区、低资源语言被合并，见 Limitations）。这是统计效率，不是任务最优组织。
  • 岛只是索引层：Table 6 反过来也说明，推理依赖深层 transformer；岛本身不能完成推断，只充当检索/压缩的先验基底。

结论

“集合岛”式组织在发现、压缩、检索、编辑四个维度上都算法高效——本质上是一种 emergent 的向量量化 + 层次索引，这也是论文 mid-point 实验和跨模型对齐能成立的结构性原因。但它作为静态、硬分区、频率偏置的组织，无法独立支撑推理与多义消解，必须与上下文动态表示互补。换句话说：岛是高效的”语义内存与索引”，而不是高效的”运行时知识表示”——论文左图把 Semantic Memory 与 Reasoning Mechanisms 画成两个模块，恰好就是这个分工的直观表达。

1. 混合高斯与软成员身份（Soft Membership）

把每个概念岛建模为一个高斯成分：

\[p(x) = \sum_{k=1}^{K} \pi_k \, \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_k, \Sigma_k)\]

对于 “bank”，我们不再问”它属于哪个岛”，而是计算后验责任度（posterior responsibility）：

\[\gamma_k(x) = \frac{\pi_k \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_k, \Sigma_k)}{\sum_j \pi_j \mathcal{N}(x \mid \mu_j, \Sigma_j)}\]

于是 “bank” 的身份变成一个连续向量：γ(bank) = [金融: 0.62, 河岸: 0.33, 倾斜: 0.05]。这正是您说的”把两个歧义表达放在同一范畴内”——它们不是被划入两个互斥的岛，而是共享同一片密度重叠区，各自携带概率质量。重叠区的后验熵 \(H(\gamma)\) 就是”含混信息量”的精确度量，硬聚类把它强制归零，而我们要把它保留并传递给下游。

2. Dirichlet 先验：给”在野党制度”一个可调旋钮

您提到用狄利克雷分布表示多重混合，这一步的妙处在于：混合权重 \(\pi\) 本身成为随机变量：

\[\pi \sim \mathrm{Dir}(\alpha), \quad \alpha = (\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_K)\]

集中参数 \(\alpha_0 = \sum_k \alpha_k\) 就是一个政治制度旋钮： - \(\alpha_0 \to 0\)：权重稀疏，赢者通吃（独裁制，硬坍缩）； - \(\alpha_0\) 大：权重均匀，多党长期共存（议会制，叠加态持久）。

1. 分布嵌入（Distributional Embeddings）取代点嵌入 词不再是一个点 \(\mu\)，而是分布 \((\mu, \Sigma)\) 或整个混合 \(\{\pi_k, \mu_k, \Sigma_k\}\)。方差/多模态本身就是”这个词有多含混”的信号。 - 代表研究：Vilnis & McCallum (ICLR 2015), “Word Representations via Gaussian Embedding”——用高斯的方差捕捉词的不确定性； - Athiwaratkun & Wilson (ACL 2017), “Multimodal Word Distributions”——直接用狄利克雷过程高斯混合表示多义词的多个义项模态。这篇几乎就是您想法的 2017 年版本：一个词 = 一个混合分布，“bank” 天然是双峰的。

2. 序贯贝叶斯消歧（Sequential Bayesian Disambiguation） 叠加态随上下文逐 token 更新，而不是在入口一次性坍缩：

\[p(s \mid x_{1:t}) \propto p(x_t \mid s) \, p(s \mid x_{1:t-1})\]

3. Chow 拒绝规则：延迟承诺（Principle of Least Commitment） 经典决策理论（Chow, 1970）给出”何时不该决策”的形式化：若 \(\max_k \gamma_k(x) < 1 - \lambda\)（拒绝阈值），则拒答/延迟/请求更多信息，而不是硬猜。映射到 LLM：当 “bank” 的后验过于平坦时，Decider 不注入锚点，而是让主模型保持叠加态，或主动反问用户（“您指的是银行还是河岸？”）。含混被保留为一种合法的决策输出。

4. 不确定性感知注意力（Uncertainty-Aware Attention） Attention 的相似度不再用点积 \(q \cdot k\)，而用分布间重叠度量——如 Bhattacharyya 系数、KL 散度或期望似然：

\[a_{ij} = \mathbb{E}_{x \sim q_i, y \sim k_j}[\, x^\top y \,] - \beta \, D_{KL}(q_i \| k_j)\]

两个”含混 token”之间的注意力会被其方差自动下调或上调，含混信息直接参与注意力计算，而不是在 softmax 前被抹平。

LLM 本身是“文科生”，做精确的 Dirichlet 数学计算容易产生“概率幻觉”（比如算出的概率加起来不等于 1）。您可以把算法打包成一个 Python 函数或 API，让 LLM 通过 Function Calling 来调用。

  • 打包方式： 写一个 Python 脚本 ambiguity_resolver.py，里面包含 MMSB 后验计算和熵门控逻辑。
  • 工作流：
    1. LLM 读到 “bank”，触发工具调用：calculate_posterior(word="bank", context="water flow")
    2. 外部 Python 引擎进行精确的矩阵运算和贝叶斯更新，返回 JSON：{"posteriors": {"finance": 0.12, "river": 0.88}, "entropy": 0.35, "status": "collapsed"}
    3. LLM 读取这个精确的数学结果，继续生成文本。
  • 搞头在哪？：这是最优雅的神经符号系统（Neuro-Symbolic）。LLM 负责模糊的语义感知（System 1），您的算法负责严谨的概率逻辑（System 2）。两者结合，既保留了 LLM 的泛化能力，又用数学锁死了幻觉。

玩法三：作为“元认知微调语料”（Meta-Cognitive SFT / 过程蒸馏）

这是目前 OpenAI (o1模型) 和 Anthropic 正在疯狂投入的方向：教模型“如何思考”，而不是仅仅教模型“答案”。

  • 打包方式： 利用您的算法，自动生成几十万条包含“歧义探索过程”的高质量语料。
    • Input: “The bank was closed due to the holiday.”
    • Output: <thought> 识别到bank。先验：[金融0.5, 河岸0.5]。证据："closed", "holiday" 强烈指向金融机构的营业时间。后验更新：[金融0.95, 河岸0.05]。熵极低，坍缩至金融岛。 </thought> 因为今天是节假日，所以金融机构没有营业。
  • 搞头在哪？：用这些语料对开源模型（如 Llama 3）进行监督微调（SFT）。微调后的模型，其内部的 Attention 机制会隐式地学会模拟您的 Dirichlet 更新过程。您不需要在推理时再输入长篇大论的算法，模型已经把“延迟坍缩”和“在野党并行”内化成了自己的“直觉”。这就是所谓的思维蒸馏（Thought Distillation）。

为什么这个思路比“修改 Transformer 底层”更聪明？

  1. 零侵入性（Zero-Intrusion）：您不需要去动 Llama 或 Qwen 那几千亿个参数，也不需要懂复杂的 CUDA 算子。您只是在应用层（Prompt/Tool/RAG） 施加了“概念空间的先验分布”。
  2. 算力经济学（Asymmetric Compute）：如果让底层 Transformer 每个词都做 Dirichlet 计算，算力会爆炸。但打包成语料或工具后，LLM 可以只在遇到高熵词（如 bank, play, right）时才触发这套算法，对于 “the”, “is” 这种低熵词直接跳过。
  3. 动态热更新：如果明天互联网上 “bank” 出了一个新梗（比如“内存 bank”），您只需要更新外部的“概念岛先验表”或 Prompt 规则，一秒钟就能让 LLM 掌握新概念，而不需要花费几百万美元去重新微调模型。

必须警惕的“坑”（Critical Challenges）

如果您要把这个想法写成论文或做成产品，必须解决以下两个问题：

  1. LLM 的“伪概率”问题： 如果您只用 Prompt 让 LLM 自己算概率，它经常会输出 [金融: 0.6, 河岸: 0.6] 这种违背概率论公理的荒谬结果。解法：必须强制 LLM 输出 JSON 格式，并调用外部代码解释器（Code Interpreter）来执行真实的数学归一化；或者在微调语料中严格过滤掉数学错误的样本。
  2. 过度思考导致的延迟（Overthinking Latency）： 如果一句话有 5 个多义词，LLM 为每个词都输出一段“贝叶斯更新过程”，用户会等得崩溃。解法：引入一个轻量级的“触发器（Trigger）”模型（类似您之前提到的 Decider），只有当 Decider 判定当前句子的“整体语义熵”超过阈值时，才唤醒这套复杂的算法语料。

二、每一步的详细操作

Step 1：歧义种子挖掘（先验从哪来）

直接复用论文管线的”软化版”，这是您这套思想和原论文最漂亮的衔接点：

  • 对基座模型（如 Qwen2.5 / Llama3 的输入嵌入）跑 K-NN + 软社区检测（MMSB 或 DPGMM，替代 Louvain），得到每个 token 的 Dirichlet 先验浓度 \(\alpha_0\) 与隶属后验 \(\pi_0\in\Delta^{K-1}\)。
  • 计算先验熵 \(H(\pi_0)\)。取 \(H(\pi_0) > 0.6\) 的 token 作为”多义枢纽种子”（预计占词表 3–8%，ALBERT 30K 词表约 1–2K 个种子）。
  • 交叉补充三个外部源：WordNet/BabelNet 的 polysemy count（sense 数 ≥ 3）、WiC 词表、SemEval WSD 标注词。
  • 产出一张先验表 prior_table.json：{token: {sense_id: π0, island_id: k}}。这张表既是语料生成的原料，也是推理时可选的检索外挂。

Step 2：三类上下文构造（教三种行为）

每个种子词 \(w\) 配三类句子，缺一不可：

类型 证据强度 期望行为 示例（bank）
A 强证据 似然极端 立即坍缩 “She deposited her paycheck into the bank account.”
B 弱/混合证据 似然接近 保留叠加、开分支、条件作答 “He waited by the bank.”
C 证据翻转 先A后B 延迟坍缩 + 在野党翻盘 “He waited by the bank, reviewing its interest rates… then watched the water swirl around its edge.”

C 类是整套语料的灵魂：没有 C，模型学不会”延迟承诺”，只会学成”晚一点的硬分类”。构造方式：强模型合成 + 从 Wiki/Books 语料检索含转折的句子，人工/校验器筛。

Step 3：Trace 生成（数字必须真，措辞可以活）

三方案混合，数字一律来自符号引擎（这是防”伪概率幻觉”的铁律）：

  • 符号引擎（Python，~200 行）：输入 (π0, 句中证据词, 每 sense 似然表)，真实计算 \(\pi \propto \pi_0 \odot L\)、\(H(\pi)=-\sum \pi_k\log\pi_k\)、门控决策、分支列表。输出 JSON skeleton。
  • 教师模型（Qwen-Plus / GPT-4o-mini 级）：把 skeleton 润色成自然语言 thought，温度 0.9、多模板，防格式复读。
  • 纯模板档（MVP 用）：不用教师，skeleton 直接转文本，零成本。

Step 4：校验器（拒绝采样规则清单）

每条 trace 过六关，任何一条不过即丢弃：

  1. \(\sum_k \pi_k = 1 \pm 10^{-3}\)；
  2. 后验与 \(\pi_0\odot L\) 逐元素比例一致（更新数学正确）；
  3. \(H(\pi)\) 与声明值一致；
  4. 门控一致性：\(H<\tau_{col}\) ⇒ 必须 COLLAPSE 且 sense=argmax；\(H>\tau_{col}\) ⇒ 必须 KEEP/branch，正文不得单义断言；
  5. C 类最终坍缩义必须与最后一段证据一致；
  6. evidence 词必须真实出现在句中（字符串匹配，防教师编证据）。

  3. Stage 3：C 类（学翻盘与剪枝）；
  4. Stage 4：一句多歧义词 + 与真实任务（QA/摘要/代码）混合，学触发器（何时启动 <amb>）。 每阶段混入 15–20% 通用指令数据（ShareGPT 级），防能力税与灾难性遗忘。

        L = [f'<amb>', f'token: "{self.token}"  H(prior)={self.prior_entropy:.2f} '
                       f'vs tau_trig={TAU_TRIGGER:.2f} -> {"run" if self.triggered else "skip"}',
             f"prior pi0: {f(self.prior)}"]
        if not self.triggered:
            L.append("decision: SKIP (low prior entropy)")
        for s in self.steps:
            L.append(f't{s.t}: evidence {s.evidence}  L: {f(s.likelihood)}')
            gate = (f"COLLAPSE {s.provisional[1]}" if s.provisional[0] == "COLLAPSE"
                    else "KEEP")
            L.append(f"    posterior: {f(s.posterior)}  H={s.entropy:.2f} -> {gate}")
        if self.decision == "KEEP_SUPERPOSITION":
            L.append("decision: KEEP_SUPERPOSITION")
            for s_, hint in self.branches.items():
                L.append(f"    branch_{s_}: {hint}")
        elif self.decision == "COLLAPSE":
            L.append(f"decision: COLLAPSE -> {self.final_sense}"
                     + ("   # flipped: opposition party took power" if self.flipped else ""))
        L.append("</amb>")
        return "\n".join(L)

class BayesianAmbiguityEngine:
    def __init__(self, tau_trigger: float = TAU_TRIGGER,
                 tau_collapse: float = TAU_COLLAPSE):
        self.tau_trigger, self.tau_collapse = tau_trigger, tau_collapse

    def _likelihood(self, entry: SensePrior, words: List[str]) -> Tuple[Dist, List[str]]:
        acc = {s: 1.0 for s in entry.prior}
        matched = []
        for w in words:
            lex = entry.evidence_lex.get(w.lower())
            if lex is None:
                continue                      # 未登录证据词: 忽略(由校验器 R6 管接地)
            matched.append(w)
            for s in acc:
                acc[s] *= lex.get(s, FLOOR_LIK)
        if not matched:                       # 无词汇证据 → 弱中性证据
            acc = {s: 1.0 for s in acc}
        return normalize(acc), matched

    def run(self, entry: SensePrior, sentence: str,
            segments: List[List[str]]) -> Trace:
        H0 = entropy_norm(entry.prior)
        if H0 <= self.tau_trigger:            # 触发门: 低熵词直接跳过
            return Trace(entry.token, sentence, entry.prior, H0, False,
                         [], "SKIP", None, {}, False)
        post, steps = dict(entry.prior), []
        for i, seg in enumerate(segments, 1):
            lik, matched = self._likelihood(entry, seg)
            post = normalize({s: post[s] * lik[s] for s in post})
            H = entropy_norm(post)
            prov = (("COLLAPSE", argmax(post)) if H < self.tau_collapse
                    else ("KEEP", None))
            steps.append(Step(i, matched, lik, post, H, prov))
        last = steps[-1]
        if last.entropy < self.tau_collapse:
            decision, final = "COLLAPSE", argmax(last.posterior)
            branches = {}
        else:                                 # 熵门控: 保留叠加 + top2 分支
            decision, final = "KEEP_SUPERPOSITION", None
            top2 = sorted(last.posterior, key=last.posterior.get, reverse=True)[:2]
            branches = {s: entry.branch_hints.get(s, f"context implies '{s}'")
                        for s in top2}
        flipped = any(st.provisional[1] is not None and final is not None
                      and st.provisional[1] != final for st in steps[:-1])
        return Trace(entry.token, sentence, entry.prior, H0, True,
                     steps, decision, final, branches, flipped)

@dataclass
class Report:
    rules: Dict[str, Tuple[bool, str]]
    @property
    def accepted(self) -> bool:
        return all(ok for ok, _ in self.rules.values())
    def __str__(self) -> str:
        return "; ".join(f"[{'PASS' if ok else 'FAIL'}] {k}: {msg}"
                         for k, (ok, msg) in self.rules.items())

class TraceValidator:
    """六关 + 先验保真关; 任一 FAIL 即拒绝采样丢弃"""
    def __init__(self, tau_trigger=TAU_TRIGGER, tau_collapse=TAU_COLLAPSE):
        self.tau_trigger, self.tau_collapse = tau_trigger, tau_collapse

    @staticmethod
    def _simplex_ok(d: Dist) -> bool:
        return abs(sum(d.values()) - 1.0) <= SUM_TOL and all(v >= 0 for v in d.values())

    def validate(self, tr: dict, entry: SensePrior) -> Report:
        R: Dict[str, Tuple[bool, str]] = {}
        need = {"token", "sentence", "prior", "prior_entropy", "steps",
                "decision", "final_sense", "branches", "flipped"}
        R["R0_schema"] = (need <= set(tr), "required fields")
        if not R["R0_schema"][0]:
            return Report(R)
        sent_l = tr["sentence"].lower()

        # R1 单纯形: prior / 每步 L / 每步 posterior
        bad = []
        if not self._simplex_ok(tr["prior"]): bad.append("prior")
        for s in tr["steps"]:
            if not self._simplex_ok(s["likelihood"]): bad.append(f"L@t{s['t']}")
            if not self._simplex_ok(s["posterior"]): bad.append(f"post@t{s['t']}")
        R["R1_simplex"] = (not bad, f"violations={bad}")

        # R2 更新一致性: post ≐ normalize(prev ⊙ L)
        prev, dev = tr["prior"], 0.0
        for s in tr["steps"]:
            exp = normalize({k: prev[k] * s["likelihood"][k] for k in prev})
            dev = max(dev, max(abs(exp[k] - s["posterior"][k]) for k in exp))
            prev = s["posterior"]
        R["R2_update"] = (dev <= UPD_TOL, f"max_dev={dev:.2e}")

        # R3 熵声明一致
        devH = abs(entropy_norm(tr["prior"]) - tr["prior_entropy"])
        for s in tr["steps"]:
            devH = max(devH, abs(entropy_norm(s["posterior"]) - s["entropy"]))
        R["R3_entropy"] = (devH <= ENT_TOL, f"max_dev={devH:.2e}")

        # R4 门控一致: 每步 provisional + 最终 decision/branches
        ok4, msg4 = True, ""
        for s in tr["steps"]:
            low = s["entropy"] < self.tau_collapse
            want = "COLLAPSE" if low else "KEEP"
            got = s["provisional"][0]
            if want != got: ok4, msg4 = False, f"t{s['t']} gate {got}!={want}"
            if low and s["provisional"][1] != argmax(s["posterior"]):
                ok4, msg4 = False, f"t{s['t']} collapse sense != argmax"
        if tr["steps"]:
            last = tr["steps"][-1]
            if last["entropy"] < self.tau_collapse:
                if not (tr["decision"] == "COLLAPSE"
                        and tr["final_sense"] == argmax(last["posterior"])):
                    ok4, msg4 = False, "final decision/sense mismatch"
            else:
                top2 = set(sorted(last["posterior"], key=last["posterior"].get,
                                  reverse=True)[:2])
                if not (tr["decision"] == "KEEP_SUPERPOSITION"
                        and top2 <= set(tr["branches"])):
                    ok4, msg4 = False, "KEEP requires top2 branches"
        elif tr["decision"] != "SKIP" or tr["prior_entropy"] > self.tau_trigger:
            ok4, msg4 = False, "skip/trigger inconsistency"
        R["R4_gating"] = (ok4, msg4 or "consistent")

        # R5 独立重算链 + 翻转标志一致 (flip = 在野党翻盘)
        prev = tr["prior"]
        for s in tr["steps"]:
            prev = normalize({k: prev[k] * s["likelihood"][k] for k in prev})
        ok5 = True
        if tr["decision"] == "COLLAPSE":
            ok5 = argmax(prev) == tr["final_sense"]
        flip_re = any(s["provisional"][1] is not None and tr["final_sense"] is not None
                      and s["provisional"][1] != tr["final_sense"]
                      for s in tr["steps"][:-1])
        ok5 = ok5 and (flip_re == tr["flipped"])
        R["R5_recompute_flip"] = (ok5, f"flipped={tr['flipped']}")

        # R6 证据接地: 证据词必须真实出现在句中
        miss = [w for s in tr["steps"] for w in s["evidence"]
                if w.lower() not in sent_l]
        R["R6_grounding"] = (not miss, f"missing={miss}")

        # R7 先验保真: trace 不得自造先验
        devp = max(abs(tr["prior"][k] - entry.prior[k]) for k in entry.prior)
        R["R7_prior_fidelity"] = (devp <= SUM_TOL, f"max_dev={devp:.2e}")
        return Report(R)

TeacherFn = Callable[[dict], str]

def build_sft_record(tr: Trace, answer: str,
                     teacher: Optional[TeacherFn] = None) -> dict:
    skeleton = tr.to_json()
    thought = teacher(skeleton) if teacher else tr.to_text()
    return {"messages": [
                {"role": "user", "content": tr.sentence},
                {"role": "assistant",
                 "content": thought + f"\n<answer>{answer}</answer>"}],
            "meta": {"token": tr.token, "decision": tr.decision,
                     "flipped": tr.flipped}}

def corrupt_simplex(tr: dict) -> dict:
    """伪概率: posterior 和 ≠ 1 → R1 FAIL"""
    t = json.loads(json.dumps(tr)); t["steps"][0]["posterior"]["finance"] += 0.3

def corrupt_gate(tr: dict) -> dict:
    """过早坍缩: 高熵却硬 COLLAPSE → R4 FAIL (rejected 类型 R1)"""
    t = json.loads(json.dumps(tr))
    t["decision"], t["final_sense"], t["branches"] = "COLLAPSE", "river", {}
    return t

def corrupt_evidence(tr: dict) -> dict:
    """编造证据: 句中不存在的词 → R6 FAIL"""
    t = json.loads(json.dumps(tr)); t["steps"][0]["evidence"].append("loan")
    return t
